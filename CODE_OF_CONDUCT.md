# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Sniff community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, and to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our Standard

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant**, version 2.1. Please read the full text
for the expected standards of behavior, enforcement responsibilities, scope, and
enforcement guidelines:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainer. For anything sensitive, use the private form at
https://github.com/Aboudjem/sniff/security/advisories/new ("Report a vulnerability" on the
Security tab). Otherwise, contact the maintainer through https://github.com/Aboudjem.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
